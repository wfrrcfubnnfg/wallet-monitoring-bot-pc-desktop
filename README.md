## 🛡️ Real talk — what monitoring can and can't do

- ⏱ **Alerts ≠ trades.** The bot tells you the dev moved LP; selling in 30 seconds vs 3 hours is on you
- 🎭 **Clever bad actors split activity across fresh wallets.** Monitoring helps — it doesn't de-anonymize chains
- 🔐 **Watching your own wallet ≠ securing it.** The approval alarm tells you something happened — a hardware wallet and revoked approvals are what prevent it
- ⚠️ **Public data has limits.** You see movements, not intentions

> ⚠️ Not financial advice. The bot shows you the cards — it doesn't play the hand.
